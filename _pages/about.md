Hi, I am Zhehao Dong. I am currently a master's student at the [College of Engineering](https://www.coe.pku.edu.cn/), [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/). In September 2024, I received my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering & Mechanics from [Sichuan University](https://www.scu.edu.cn/). During my academic journey, I gained practical experience as an NLP/VLM algorithm intern at [Baidu AI Cloud](https://cloud.baidu.com/). I also joined [GigaAI](https://gigaai.cc/) as a VLA/World Model Research Intern, where I focused on World Model to bridge the Sim-to-Real gap and Real-to-Real gap in robotic systems, and investigated high-efficiency VLA training strategies to boost real-world deployment performance.

My research interests lie in **Large Language Model**,  **AI agents**, and **Embodied Intelligence**. 

You can find my résumé here: [Zhehao Dong's Résumé](http://vittoriodong.github.io/zhehaodong.github.io/assets/resume.pdf).
